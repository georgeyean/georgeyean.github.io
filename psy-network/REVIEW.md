# Political psychology, c. 1950–2026: where it went, what settled, what is open

A narrative companion to `graph.json` and `network.html` in this folder. The graph has 326 work nodes, 18 theme nodes and 337 typed relations. Each theme section follows the same order: **what was claimed → what the evidence settled → what is contested → gaps and research opportunities**. Node ids in brackets (e.g. `[kunda1990]`) point to entries in `graph.json`.

**How the works were chosen.**
- **Shared works:** 133 works already in other maps are reused. Most come from the American politics map (opinion, motivated reasoning, emotion, personality, genes, misinformation and partisan identity). Others come from REP (racial attitudes), security and IR (leaders' psychology), technology (misinformation) and comparative politics (authoritarian values).
- **Top-cited articles:** 141 come from the most-cited 2006–2026 articles in *Political Psychology*, the *Journal of Experimental Political Science*, *JPSP* and *Psychological Science* (political articles only). Psychology-related articles in APSR, AJPS, JOP, BJPS, *Political Behavior*, POQ, *Political Communication*, ARPS, ISQ, IO and *Perspectives on Politics* were added too.
- **Anchors:** 52 books and classic articles, from Adorno et al. and Festinger to Kunda, Marcus, Jost, Haidt and Lewandowsky. 46 matched Crossref directly; 6 books exist there only through reviews (see the end).

Relations are judgments of how each work's argument relates to the other's, and every relation runs from the later work to the earlier one. One-line claims for non-library works are my summaries.

## 1. Belief systems and political knowledge
**Claimed.** Most citizens lack constrained belief systems `[converse1964]`; opinion is a sample of considerations `[zaller1992]`. **Settled.** Knowledge is low and unequal but consequential `[dellicarpini1996]`. **Contested.** How to measure it `[priorlupia2008]`, `[luskinbullock2011]`, `[barabas2014]`. **Gaps.** Knowledge in fragmented media environments `[prior2007]`.

## 2. Motivated reasoning
**Settled.** Directional goals bias evaluation `[kunda1990]`, `[taberlodge2006]`, `[lodgetaber2013]`, and party cues intensify this `[bolsen2014]`, `[druckman2013]`. **Contested.** Bias versus Bayesian updating `[bullock2009]`; sincere versus expressive gaps `[bullocklenz2019]`, `[petersoniyengar2020]`. **Gaps.** When motivated reasoners update `[redlawsk2010]`.

## 3. Emotion
**Settled.** Anxiety prompts attention and learning `[marcusmackuen1993]`, `[marcus2000]`; discrete emotions differ `[lernerkeltner2001]`, `[huddy2005]`. **Contested.** Anger's role in participation and bias `[valentino2011]`, `[suhayerisen2018]`. **Gaps.** Emotion and authoritarian voting `[vasilopoulos2018]`, `[marcus2019]`.

## 4. Personality and values
**Settled.** Big Five traits and basic values predict attitudes and participation `[mondakhalperin2008]`, `[gerber2010]`, `[schwartz1992]`. **Contested.** Causal direction and shared genes `[verhulst2011]`. **Gaps.** Cross-national variation `[fatke2016]`.

## 5. Authoritarianism and threat
**Claimed.** An authoritarian personality underlies prejudice `[adorno1950]`. **Settled.** Authoritarianism as a predisposition activated by threat `[feldmanstenner1997]`, `[stenner2005]`, distinct from social dominance `[sidaniuspratto1999]`. **Contested.** Left-wing authoritarianism `[conway2017]`. **Gaps.** Authoritarian populism `[norris2019]`, `[malka2020]`.

## 6. Social and partisan identity
**Settled.** Partisanship is a social identity `[greenpalmquist2002]`, `[huddy2015]`, `[mason2018]`. **Contested.** Whether identities follow politics `[egan2019]`. **Gaps.** Identity in multiparty systems `[huddy2018]`, `[bankert2016]`.

## 7. Affective polarization
**Settled.** Out-party animus has grown `[iyengar2012]`, `[iyengarwestwood2015]`, `[iyengarkrupenkin2018]`. **Contested.** Identity versus ideology `[rogowskisutherland2016]`, `[diaslelkes2021]`; measurement `[druckman2022]`; consequences for democracy `[kingzette2021]`, `[broockman2022]`. **Gaps.** Comparative trends `[garzia2023]`, `[gidron2022]`.

## 8. Framing, priming and persuasion
**Settled.** Frames change which considerations matter `[nelson1997]`, `[chongdruckman2007]`; credible sources are required `[druckman2001]`. **Contested.** Priming versus learning `[lenz2009]`; durability `[hill2013]`. **Gaps.** Durable persuasion through conversation `[kallabroockman2020]`.

## 9. Heuristics and elite cues
**Settled.** Shortcuts help `[lupia1994]`, but unevenly `[lauredlawsk2001]`. **Contested.** Party over policy `[cohen2003]` versus policy information `[bullock2011]`.

## 10. Misinformation and conspiracy beliefs
**Settled.** Many are misinformed, not uninformed `[kuklinski2000]`; misinformation persists `[lewandowsky2012]`. **Contested.** The backfire effect `[nyhanreifler2010]`, `[woodporter2019]`; lazy versus partisan sharing `[pennycookrand2019]`, `[osmundsen2021]`. **Gaps.** AI-generated misinformation `[kreps2020]`.

## 11. Moral foundations
**Settled.** Different moral foundations by ideology `[graham2009]`, `[haidt2012]`. **Contested.** Causal order `[smith2017]`.

## 12. Ideology and the psychology of left and right
**Claimed.** Conservatism as motivated social cognition `[jost2003]`. **Contested.** Asymmetry `[jost2017]` versus symmetry `[crawfordpilanski2012]`, `[toner2013]`; context dependence `[federicomalka2018]`.

## 13. Biology and genetics
**Claimed.** Orientations are partly heritable `[alford2005]` and linked to physiology `[oxley2008]`. **Contested.** Twin-study assumptions `[charney2008]`. **Gaps.** Evolutionary mechanisms `[petersen2011]`.

## 14. Trust and tolerance
**Settled.** Intolerance is widespread `[stouffer1955]`, `[sullivan1982]`, `[gibson2006]`; trust shapes policy support `[hetherington2005]`. **Gaps.** Democratic hypocrisy `[simonovits2022]`.

## 15. Socialization
**Settled.** Parents transmit party identity `[jenningsniemi1968]`, `[jennings2009]`. **Gaps.** Genes versus upbringing `[hatemi2009]`.

## 16. Leaders and international politics
**Settled.** Perception shapes policy `[jervis1976]`; prospect theory applies `[levy1997]`. **Contested.** Elite versus mass reasoning `[kertzerrenshon2022]`. **Gaps.** Emotion in IR `[mercer2010]`.

## 17. Intergroup attitudes
Symbolic racism and ethnocentrism `[kindersears1981]`, `[kinderkam2009]` link to REP; collective narcissism `[golecdezavala2019]`.

## 18. Experiments and measurement
Experiments now dominate `[druckman2006]` and generalize across samples `[mullinix2015]`.
