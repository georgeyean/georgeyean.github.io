How to read the arrows

An arrow runs from the later or dependent work to the work it relates to: A → B means "A builds on / challenges / qualifies B". Hover an arrow for the reason; click a node to list all of its arrows with reasons.

builds on: extends or relies on B's argument or evidence
challenges: contests B's claim, mechanism or evidence
qualifies: adds a scope condition or moderator to B
extends to new case: applies B to a new country, period or domain
synthesizes: integrates B with other work (reviews, frameworks)
mechanism tested by: tests a mechanism B implied
thin line, no arrowhead: work → its theme hub (no relation)
